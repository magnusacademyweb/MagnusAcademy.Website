NEET guide for Tamil Nadu students

Tamil Nadu NEET cutoff 2026 for government MBBS colleges

By Magnus Academy, Hosur · Updated October 2026

The short answer

In Round 1 of Tamil Nadu state counselling for 2026, Madras Medical College closed at state rank 70 and Stanley Medical College at 247. Most government medical colleges filled up within roughly the top 1,400 state ranks on the general list in Round 1. In marks, the 2025 Round 1 closing score at Madras Medical College was about 609 for OC and 591 for BC, while newer district colleges closed around 500–517 for BC. Reserved categories and the 7.5% government-school quota close at lower marks.

How MBBS admission works in Tamil Nadu

Your NEET score is used in two separate counselling processes:

  • All India Quota (15%) of seats in government medical colleges is filled through national counselling by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), open to students from every state.
  • State quota (85%) of government college seats, plus the government-quota seats in private (self-financing) colleges, is filled by the Tamil Nadu Selection Committee under the Directorate of Medical Education. This is where most Tamil Nadu students get their seats.

State counselling follows Tamil Nadu's 69% reservation: BC 26.5%, BC (Muslim) 3.5%, MBC/DNC 20%, SC 18% (including 3% for Arunthathiyar), and ST 1%, with the rest open to all. On top of this, 7.5% of government seats are reserved for students who studied in Tamil Nadu government schools from Class 6 to Class 12.

The Selection Committee publishes a merit list ranked by NEET marks. For 2026–27, the government-quota rank list had 35,926 candidates, with marks ranging from 705 down to 177.

What these ranks mean in marks

Closing marks depend on your community, so there's no single cutoff. Based on the 2025 Round 1 allotment lists:

  • Top colleges: Madras Medical College closed at about 609 marks for OC and 591 for BC.
  • Newer district government colleges: about 500–517 marks for BC and 441–465 for SC.
  • 7.5% government-school quota: much lower. Madras Medical College closed around 498 (BC) and 492 (SC), and newer colleges around 409–446.

The 2026 Re-NEET was scored more generously than 2025, so analysts estimate that a 2025 closing mark corresponds to a mark roughly 5–9 points higher in 2026. Treat these as guides, not guarantees, and compare your state rank and community rank, not just your marks.

Seats and competition are changing

Tamil Nadu now has 13,999 MBBS seats across government and private colleges. Government MBBS seats rose from 5,200 in 2025 to 5,349 in 2026, and private seats from 7,800 to 8,650. At the same time, the number of Tamil Nadu students who qualified NEET fell from 76,181 in 2025 to 61,306 in 2026. More seats and fewer qualified candidates can loosen closing ranks in later rounds.

What this means for your preparation

  • Aim for a buffer. If your target college closed around a certain mark last year, aim 20–30 marks above it to stay safe from year-to-year changes.
  • Know your category and quota. Your community and, if eligible, the 7.5% government-school quota can change your options significantly. Keep certificates ready well before counselling.
  • Don't ignore later rounds. Seats open up in Round 2 and mop-up rounds as students move to other colleges.
  • Consider all options. Government-quota seats in private colleges, BDS and AYUSH courses such as BSMS (Siddha) also use NEET scores.

Frequently asked questions

What is the NEET cutoff for a government MBBS seat in Tamil Nadu?

It depends on your community and the college. In 2025 Round 1, top colleges closed around 609 (OC) and 591 (BC), while newer government colleges closed around 500–517 for BC and 441–465 for SC. 2026 marks run a few points higher because the Re-NEET scored higher.

Can I get MBBS in Tamil Nadu with 500 marks?

Possibly, depending on your community and quota. Around 500 marks, students from some reserved categories or the 7.5% government-school quota can get government seats, and government-quota seats in private colleges are often possible. Check your state rank and community rank against last year's allotments.

Who is eligible for the 7.5% government school quota?

Students who studied in Tamil Nadu state government schools continuously from Class 6 to Class 12 and qualified NEET. A certificate from the school, countersigned by the education officer, is required.

Should I apply for All India Quota as well as state counselling?

Yes. Registering for both gives you more chances. Many Tamil Nadu students register for MCC counselling too, especially those with higher scores.
